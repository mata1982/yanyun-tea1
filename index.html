THE CURATOR'S WORD

We don't grow tea.
We select it.好茶是选出来的。

For thirty years, we've walked the same narrow paths through Wuyi's mist-covered valleys — the legendary Hui Yuan Keng, Niu Lan Keng, and Da Keng Kou. We knock on the same wooden doors. We drink tea with the same families.

The farmer who grows our Reserve old pine was eight years old when his grandfather planted that tree. The man who charcoal roasts our Da Hong Pao has been doing it since 1976. His hands know exactly when the leaves are ready — a skill that cannot be taught, only inherited.

We don't sell a hundred labels. We select two teas each year — the absolute best. If we can't find a worthy batch, we don't release it. That's happened twice in thirty years.
