Three Generations
One Cup of Tea三代人的一杯茶

岩韵茶庄始于一九九零年，坐落在武夷山正岩产区的核心地带。三代茶人，传承着从清末延续至今的炭焙工艺。

"岩韵，是山场在茶杯里的回响。直接贸易，意味着每一饼茶都可追溯至它生长的岩壁。"

BREWING GUIDE

冲泡指南

Unlock the full flavor of Wuyi rock tea with the traditional gongfu method.

1

Warm the Vessel

Rinse your gaiwan or teapot with boiling water to bring it to temperature.

2

Add Leaves

5g of tea per 100ml of water. For rock tea, don't be shy — the leaves need room to open.

3

Quick Rinse

Pour 100°C water over the leaves and immediately discard. This 'awakens' the tea.

4

Steep & Enjoy

First steep: 15 seconds. Add 5 seconds to each subsequent steep. Wuyi rock tea rewards patience — expect 7+ infusions.

FAQ

常见问题

How long does Wuyi rock tea stay fresh?+
Properly stored in an airtight container away from light, moisture, and strong odors, Wuyi rock tea can stay fresh for 2-3 years. The charcoal roasting process helps preserve it longer than green teas. Many connoisseurs find that rock tea improves with 1-2 years aging.
What temperature water should I use?+
Always use freshly boiled water at 100°C (212°F) for Wuyi rock tea. Unlike green teas, rock teas need near-boiling water to fully release their complex mineral and roast flavors. Lower temperatures won't properly extract the 'yan yun' character.
How many infusions can I get from one serving?+
You can typically get 7-10 infusions from a single 5g serving using the gongfu method. Each steep reveals a different facet: the first steeps are bold and roasted, middle steeps are floral and sweet, later steeps become delicate and mineral.

Da Hong Pao is the legendary king — complex and mineral. Rou Gui (Cinnamon) is bold and spicy. Shui Xian (Narcissus) is the most approachable — smooth and floral. Try the Sampler Set to discover your favorite!
