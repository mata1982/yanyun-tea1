Privacy Policy 隐私政策

Last updated: May 2025

Yan Yun Tea ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website and make purchases.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information You Provide

  • Account information: name, email address, shipping address, phone number
  • Payment information: We do not store your credit card details. All payments are processed securely through Stripe, a PCI Level 1 certified payment processor
  • Communications: Any messages you send us through our contact form or email

Information Collected Automatically

  • Cookies & tracking: We use minimal cookies for cart functionality and basic analytics
  • Usage data: Pages visited, time spent, referring website (anonymized where possible)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Process and fulfill your orders
  • Send you order confirmations and shipping updates
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Send occasional marketing emails (only if you opt in — you can unsubscribe anytime)
  • Improve our website and product offerings

3. Payment Security

All payments on our site are processed by Stripe. Your credit card number is encrypted and transmitted directly to Stripe's PCI-compliant servers. We never see, store, or have access to your full card details.

We also implement:

  • SSL/TLS encryption — all data between your browser and our server is encrypted
  • 256-bit encryption — industry standard for data in transit

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Stripe — payment processing (their privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy)
  • Shipping carriers — for order delivery (name, address, phone)
  • Vercel — website hosting (their privacy policy: vercel.com/privacy)

5. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

If you are in the EU or California, you have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Opt out of data collection for marketing
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@yanyuntea.com.

6. Data Retention

We retain your order information for 5 years (for tax and accounting purposes). Marketing preferences are kept until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is anonymized after 26 months.

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies for cart functionality (localStorage). We do not use third-party tracking cookies without your consent. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect your shopping experience.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Material changes will be notified via email.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us:

  • Email: privacy@yanyuntea.com
  • We reply within 24 hours
