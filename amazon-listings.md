### Product Description

**The Tea That Changed the World**

Lapsang Souchong (Zheng Shan Xiao Zhong) is not merely a tea — it is a piece of history. Created by accident in the Wuyi Mountains during the late Ming Dynasty (circa 1600s), this was the very first black tea ever produced. When an army delayed the tea harvest, desperate producers rushed the drying process by firing the leaves over pinewood, discovering a bold, smoky character that would captivate the Western world.

The tea became so popular in Europe that it launched the global black tea trade, eventually leading to the development of English breakfast teas and the tea cultures of India and Sri Lanka. Today, authentic Lapsang Souchong — Zheng Shan Xiao Zhong or "Proper Mountain Little Species" — is protected by its geographic origin, produced only in the core Wuyi Mountain保护区 where the tradition began.

**Craftsmanship**

Our Lapsang Souchong is made from the original Xiao Zhong (small leaf) varietal, hand-picked from old bushes on our family estate. After full oxidation — which transforms the green leaves into glossy black needles — the tea is dried over smoldering pinewood fires in traditional bamboo baskets. The pine smoke penetrates the leaves slowly, infusing them with a deep, complex smokiness that is bold but never harsh.

Unlike cheaper imitations that use liquid smoke flavoring, our traditional method produces a tea with remarkable nuance: the initial campfire smokiness gives way to notes of dried longan, dark honey, and pine resin, with a surprisingly sweet, clean finish.

**Tasting Profile:**
- Aroma: Rich campfire smoke, pine resin, dried fruit, dark chocolate
- Flavor: Bold smoky intensity, followed by sweet dried fruit, malt, and a hint of cedar
- Finish: Long, warming, with a sweet smoky aftertaste that lingers
- Caffeine: Moderate (black tea level)

**Brewing Guide:**
- Western: 3g leaf / 250ml water at 95°C / steep 2-3 minutes / 2 infusions
- Gongfu: 5g leaf / 100ml water at 95°C / 10-15 second steeps / 5+ infusions
- Iced: 4g leaf / 500ml water / hot brew double strength, pour over ice
- Pairing: Smoked salmon, barbecue, dark chocolate, aged gouda
