## 产品1：Da Hong Pao 大红袍

**Title:**
Yan Yun Da Hong Pao Wuyi Rock Tea | Traditional Charcoal Roasted Oolong Loose Leaf | Single Origin Zhengyan Region | Rich Mineral Flavor with Orchid & Honey Notes | 50g (1.76 oz) Resealable Pouch

**Bullet Points:**

1. **THE KING OF WUYI TEA** — Da Hong Pao (Big Red Robe) is the most legendary Wuyi rock tea, prized for its extraordinary mineral complexity and lingering "yan yun" (rock rhyme) aftertaste. Grown in the Zhengyan core region of Wuyi Mountain, UNESCO World Heritage site.

2. **TRADITIONAL CHARCOAL ROAST** — Slow-roasted over lychee wood charcoal for 72 hours by master roasters. This traditional technique, passed down through generations, gives Da Hong Pao its signature depth, orchid fragrance, and honey sweetness.

3. **CURATED & LAB TESTED** — Every batch is blind-tasted by our team and independently SGS tested for 400+ pesticides and heavy metals. We personally select each lot from trusted Wuyi farming families since 1990.

4. **EXPERT BREWING** — Use 5g per 100ml at 100°C (212°F). Quick rinse, then steep 15s for first infusion. Da Hong Pao rewards patience — expect 7-10 infusions, each revealing a new layer of flavor.

5. **FRESHLY PACKED** — 50g (1.76 oz) in a resealable, light-blocking pouch with nitrogen flush for freshness. 7-10 gongfu sessions per pouch.
